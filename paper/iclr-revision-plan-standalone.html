Recommended scope

A focused revision centered on the experiments

The paper already has a clear progression: audio dependence varies across tokens; MAPO adjusts token weighting and attention; experiments evaluate the resulting policy. The new evidence fits that progression. Repeated scores strengthen the performance comparison, branch and POS ablations explain the design, and attention plus perturbation analyses examine how the trained policy uses audio.

I would retain the original Ablation results → Main results order and add a short Audio use during reasoning subsection. The prose should lead from each result to the next scientific question. Details about sampling, uncertainty and experimental scope belong next to the corresponding protocol, with the complete records in the appendix.

Main paper

Two result tables with consistent scores, the two existing central figures, and one compact new grounding figure. Preserve the reference and Phase-1 comparisons. Add two or three connected analysis paragraphs.

Appendix

Retain category breakdowns, training curves and qualitative examples. Add full replicate records, POS-gate results, other perturbation cohorts, shared-prefix continuations, correlation analysis and implementation costs.

The current anonymous PDF already has nine main pages. Consolidating the two external-system tables and moving the small attention-loss trajectory to the appendix should create space. The exact fit will be checked after each section; this plan does not assume a completed layout.

Prose and logical flow

Let the scientific argument carry the qualifications

The earlier integration often alternates a finding with a statement about what it does not establish. Its question-style analysis headings also make the experiments sound like responses to objections. I would retain the necessary precision, but build each paragraph around a scientific connection: what we observe, why the next experiment follows, and what that experiment shows.

In the method, one clear definition can establish that attention is a routing measure and the entropy contrast is a relevance signal. In the setup, one paragraph can define uncertainty and the evaluation protocol. The result paragraphs can then describe the evidence directly. A limitation should sit beside the inference it qualifies, rather than recur in the introduction, contribution list and conclusion.

Illustration of the intended connection between paragraphs, not replacement manuscript text:

Audio contributes unevenly to a reasoning trace. Predicting an acoustic event or distinguishing between plausible interpretations may depend on the recording, while connective text can often be inferred from the preceding words. This motivates a policy objective that gives different weights to different tokens. MAPO derives those weights from the entropy contrast between the multimodal policy and a frozen text-only reference evaluated on the same generated prefix.

Reweighting the policy loss addresses how updates are distributed across the response. A complementary issue arises as the model moves from describing the audio to reasoning over its own description: direct attention to the recording may decline. We therefore add an attention penalty whose weight increases later in the response, encouraging continued access to the source while the model develops its answer.

  • Preserve useful original sentences and transitions. Edit the sentences whose evidence or logical role has changed.
  • Give the new analysis a descriptive title such as “Audio use during reasoning.” Present the experiments in their scientific order.
  • Replace causal overstatements with the observed relationship: equal loss coefficients, increased late audio attention, higher benchmark accuracy, or greater perturbation sensitivity.
  • Keep changes in performance distinct from proof of a mechanism. Longer reasoning and larger attention values are measurements, not definitions of better reasoning.

Evidence-backed

Entropy and attention need a narrower connection

For full MAPO on the 40 selected MMAU examples, mean-head token correlation is 0.079 [0.024, 0.140], and −0.038 [−0.075, 0.002] after controlling response position. The original statement that the signals “strongly correlate” at the token level therefore needs revision. Present the entropy contrast as the weighting signal and the attention loss as a complementary mechanism. Keep the cross-example association and its controls in the appendix. E5.

Evidence-backed / interpretation · limited audio dependence

The behavioral intervals add useful information

The random-MMSU primary summary contains paired uncertainty omitted from the integrated text. Its mismatch interval includes zero. On the 107 items both models answer correctly with clean audio, excess MAPO drops are +8.41, 0.00 and +3.74 points for silence, mismatch and removal, and all three intervals include zero. Report this sensitivity analysis beside the complete perturbation results in the appendix; a larger aggregate drop alone does not establish stronger causal grounding. E4.
